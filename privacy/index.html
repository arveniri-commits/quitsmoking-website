Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Quit Smoking ("the App") is designed to help users track and support their progress toward quitting smoking. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

Information We Collect

The App may collect information that you provide when using its features, such as information related to your quit-smoking progress, goals, and app preferences.

The App may also use third-party services necessary to provide certain features, including subscription and purchase management. These services may process technical information required to operate their services.

How We Use Information

Information is used to provide and improve the App's functionality, save your progress and preferences, and manage features such as subscriptions or purchases where applicable.

Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party service providers, including RevenueCat, for managing in-app purchases and subscriptions. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate the App and its features.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information associated with the App. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
