Delete Your Quit Smoking Account

This page explains how users of the Quit Smoking app can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to request account deletion

To request deletion of your Quit Smoking account, send an email to:

Email: arveniri@gmail.com

Please use the subject line "Delete Quit Smoking Account" and send the request from the email address associated with your account.

What will be deleted

After your request is verified, your Quit Smoking account and personal data associated with that account will be deleted.

This may include account information and app data associated with your user account.

Data retention

Some information may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or regulatory purposes. Any retained information will only be kept for as long as necessary for those purposes.

Processing time

Account deletion requests will normally be processed within 30 days after the request has been verified.

Contact
